"Gangs of Ireland" was an experimental sound collage project inspired by a 1983 Irish television talk show featuring interviews with young people from goth, mod, and punk subcultures. Every International Drone Day for five successive years starting in 2021, I created and performed a new composition exploring one of the interviews.

The final performance took place at the Drone Day Festival on a farm in the Hudson Valley of upstate New York on May 24, 2025.

You can hear the complete "Gangs of Ireland" collection on SoundCloud.
