This International Drone Day, I joined with my long-time collaborator Kevin B. Chatham under the banner of The Disaster Society for a livestreamed performance. We played four original compositions featuring vintage spoken samples, Moog analog synth, ebow, guitar, slide steel, electric bass, and an awful lot of guitar pedals.

The full video of the performance includes a bonus short film, "Rejected Lines". You can download the tracks for close listening or remixing from Bandcamp, available under a Creative Commons CC-BY-NC-SA license.
