  • About Me

    I'm a lifelong musician, and I am capable of making what I want with piano, synth, bass, electric and acoustic guitars, slide steel, and electric violin. I know some theory but I'm an intuitive player. I take a narrative approach to instrumental music, telling a story within a sonic landscape. Despite my best efforts, my music tends to come out dark and moody, and sometimes unnerving. Sorry about that. My work was described by Hard Times as "haunted but also uplifting". I'll take it.
